1916:

The Denison Rural High School was established and built on the west side of Central Avenue in the middle of the block between 5th and 6th Streets (Ref 70) [39°23'44.6"N 95°37'40.5"W].


Oct 4, 1917:

The Holton Recorder reported that work had begun on the new Denison High School building expected to cost $25,000. A Mr. Norris was the contractor.
