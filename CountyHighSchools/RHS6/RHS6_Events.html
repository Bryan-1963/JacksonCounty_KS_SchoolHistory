5/27/1895:

The third annual commencement of Circleville High School was held at the M.E. Church. There were two graduates from the High School Course and ten from the Common School Course (Ref 46).


1950:

Circleville’s mascot was ’The Bombers.’


7/1/1966:

Circleville, Soldier, Grant Union, Netawaka and Whiting consolidated into USD 335 Jackson Heights. Some grade school classes continued in Circleville until 1975 (Ref 46).


1967:

The last Circleville High School class graduated (Ref 46).


1968 & 1969:

The graduating classes of Circleville students for these years attended and graduated from Soldier High School. Some students opted to attend school outside the district (Ref 46).


1970:

This was the first year that Circleville students graduated from Jackson Heights High School (Ref 46).
