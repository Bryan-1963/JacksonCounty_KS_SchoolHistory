1910:

Clayton Calvin Stauffer (kneeling front row, 3rd from left, born 1894) at Circleville High School. This is in front of the white limestone seminary building.


1911:

The brick high school was built (Ref 11). It had a semi-basement and two floors above that. High School was held on the top floor plus two classrooms in the semi-basement for typing and health/home economics. A shop and restrooms were added to the north side of the high school sometime after 1911. The middle floor was the elementary school. The basement also had the cafeteria and the furnace room and provided access to the restrooms and shop (Ref 13). This was a combo of elementary and high school – when did it officially become a Rural High School??





1929:

The gymnasium was built (Ref 11). The brick high school and gymnasium were used until school unification in 1967 (Ref 11). The gymnasium continues to be used for community events to this day.




2025:

No remains of the schoolhouse on Seminary Street are visible today (Ref Google Maps 2025).
