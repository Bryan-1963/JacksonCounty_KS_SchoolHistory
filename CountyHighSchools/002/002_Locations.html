1875:

Reference 85 says the high school at 420 W 4th Street was built around 1875, but no maps prior to 1903 show a school at that location. Reference 85 also says Central School was built between 4th and 5th Streets on New Jersey Avenue in this year (Ref 85). Likely the Central School was a combination of elementary and high schools (see Rural School District #2 for information on the Holton Central School).

1905:

Fire Insurance maps for this year showed the High School on the northeast corner of 4th and Kansas. The High School was two stories tall, constructed of brick, was heated by steam, had no lights, had a slate or tin roof and no outhouses shown. Note that the High School data was taken ’from plans’ rather than physical inspection of the building (Ref 84).

1912:

Fire Insurance maps for this year showed the High School on the northeast corner of 4th and Kansas. The High School was two stories tall, constructed of brick, was heated by steam, had no lights, had a slate or tin roof and no outhouses shown (Ref 84).



1913:

Campbell College closed and sometime after this Holton High School was relocated to the Campbell College building (Ref 85).



1921:

The 1921 map of Holton shows the Senior High School taking up about two city blocks on the former Campbell University site on the north side of 9th Street between Iowa and New Your (Ref10). [39°28'14.4"N 95°44'14.7"W]


1922:

Fire Insurance maps for this year showed the High School at the former Campbell College site. The High School was two stories tall with a ½ basement, constructed of stone, had a slate or tin roof, was heated by steam and had electric lighting. The building was the former Campbell College main building (Ref 84).
