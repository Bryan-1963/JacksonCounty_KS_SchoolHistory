1887:

The Prairie View schoolhouse was shown on the map in western Grant Township on the west side of what is now E Road almost ½ mile south of 198 Road (Ref 15) [39°23'53.4"N 95°57'40.7"W].


1903:

The Prairie View schoolhouse was shown on the map in western Grant Township on the west side of what is now E Road almost ½ mile south of 198 Road (Ref 12) [39°23'53.4"N 95°57'40.7"W].


????:

The schoolhouse was moved nearly four miles south to the west side of E Road and a little over ¼ mile south of 166 Road (Ref 13) [39°20'33.4"N 95°57'41.4"W].


2025:

No remains are visible on modern aerial maps at the 1887-1903 location. (Ref Google Maps 2025).
