Spring 1903:

Reference 41 contains photos of the 1902-1903 Souvenir book.

The teacher was Anna L. Hittle and George E. Whitcraft was the County Superintendent. Note that Anna Hittle was a student at Banner in the 1900-1901 school year and was listed on the 1901 graduation program at Olive Hill. Pupils were: Josephine Edwards, Ada Edwards, Mary Mannell, Cressie Spangler, George Rings, Earl Hittle, Hobart Carpenter, Reuben Adams, Charlie Mannell, Floyd Spangler, George Fickel, John Whitcraft, Francis Whitcraft, Freddie Adams, Catherine Linehan, Ruth Taylor. Note that Catherine Linehan would later be the teacher at Pleasant Valley school.
