Bud Stauffer remembers going to a square dance here. The music was played on a record player and a live person did the "calling." The desks were mounted on boards so that they could easily be slid to the side of the room.(Ref 13)


1959:

Part of this district was absorbed into #8 when Winding Vale dissolved in 1959.

Auswell & Vinita Stauffer bought the furnace and propane tank as part of the sale of the furnishings and the land and building reverted to their ownership.(Ref 13). Auswell stored antiques in the schoolhouse until he moved them to a new Morton building at their home place in the 1970s (Ref 68).
