Carmel School ca 1916? (Ref 3) and in July 2025 at Relocation for Carwood School (Ref 68)


“Black’s School” or “Black School” district #28 was organized in 1864 in eastern Jefferson Township. The school was in a log cabin that burned down in January 1873. The district built a new schoolhouse in a new location and started classes in it in October 1874. At that point, the school was renamed to “Carmel” though the new name and “Black” or “Black’s” continued to be used interchangeably for decades. The third schoolhouse was built in 1903 and a basement and furnace were added in 1914. There were no classes held at Carmel after the spring of 1944. School district funds were used to pay tuition and transportation for students to attend school in Holton, Circleville or Glenwood until 1950. Carmel and #86 Glenwood merged in 1950 to form District #103 Carwood. The Carmel schoolhouse was relocated to a location more central to the merged school districts and still stands there today.
