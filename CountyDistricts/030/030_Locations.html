1878:

The map shows a school in northeastern Cedar Township on the east side of what is now U Road about ¼ mile south of 198 Road (KS16) (Ref 78) [39°24'03.4"N 95°39'48.9"W].


Figure 1 – 1878 Map with Dodson Hill School #30 (Ref 78)


1881:

Dodson Hill schoolhouse was shown on the map in northern Cedar Township at the southeast corner of what is now the intersection of 198 Road (KS 116 Highway) and U Road (Ref 14) [39°24'16.3"N 95°39'49.3"W].


1887:

Dodson Hill schoolhouse was shown on the map in northern Cedar Township at the southeast corner of what is now the intersection of 198 Road (KS 116 Highway) and U Road (Ref 15) [39°24'16.3"N 95°39'49.3"W].


1903:

Dodson Hill schoolhouse was shown on the map in Garfield Township at the southeast corner of what is now the intersection of 198 Road (KS 116 Highway) and U Road (Ref 12) [39°24'16.3"N 95°39'49.3"W]..


2025:

Today there is only a marker for the school location (Ref Google Maps 2025) [39°24'16.3"N 95°39'49.3"W].
