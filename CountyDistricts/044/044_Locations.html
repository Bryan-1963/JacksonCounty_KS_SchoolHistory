1881:

The Banks schoolhouse was shown on the map in Whiting Township on the west side of U Road a little over ¼ mile north of 302 Road, just south of the railroad tracks (Ref 14). [39°35'57.1"N 95°39'29.9"W]


1887:

The Banks schoolhouse was shown on the map in Whiting Township on the south side of 302 Road (Hwy 9) almost 1/4 mile west of U Road (Ref 15). [39°35'40.4"N 95°39'43.3"W]


1903:

The Banks schoolhouse was shown on the map in Whiting Township on the south side of 302 Road (Hwy 9) about 1/8 mile west of U Road (Ref 12). [39°35'41.0"N 95°39'36.4"W]
