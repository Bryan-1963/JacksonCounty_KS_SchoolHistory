Apr 29, 1959:

The Atchison Daily Globe reported that teachers and students from Grant Union School in Jackson County had visited Atchison for a field trip. A picture of the class was included, but the digital scan is a very poor copy. In the picture were:

From Holton: Kenneth Oden, Hugh Oden, Carol Oden, Ronald Oden, Bessie Oden, C.W. Beightel, Anna Beightel, Marian Beightel

From Soldier: Steve Bennett, Ilene Bennett, Mable Harris, Clinton Harris, Yvonne Harris, Pat Kennedy, Mike Kennedy, Jean Kennedy, Tommy Kennedy, Hazel Kennedy, Elizabeth, Joe Kennedy, Evelyn Slocum, Walter Slocum, Robert Fox.

From Circleville: Tim Morris and Edith Morris (teacher)


Bud Stauffer said that Edith Morris had taught at Pleasant Valley (long before 1959) and later at Sunny Slope School. The Beightels listed above lived 1.5 miles north, the Bennetts 1 mile west and the Slocums ½ mile south of Sunny Slope schoolhouse. Need to ask him about the names in green above.


The 1963 Farm Directory shows all of the 1959 student names within a mile of Sunny Slope Schoolhouse. Edith Cole (Morris)’s obituary lists Sunny Slope and Pleasant Valley as being the only rural schools she taught at.
