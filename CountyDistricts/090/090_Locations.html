1918:

Rock Brook schoolhouse was located on the west side of what is now L Road less than 1/8th mile north of 198 Road (Ref 27, Ref 13, Google Maps 2025). The street view shows a newer home on the site and a stone retention wall along the road that could be a remnant of the school grounds (Ref Google Maps 2025).






Jan 27, 1921:

Farm sale notice in the Holton Recorder relative to Rock Brook and Holton places the schoolhouse at about (sale was 6 ½ miles west and 5 miles south of Holton and was 1 mile south and 1 ½ miles west of Rock Brook School). This corresponds to the 198 Road and L Road.
