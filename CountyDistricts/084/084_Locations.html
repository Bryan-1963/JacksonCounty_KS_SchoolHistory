1878:

The map shows a school on the north side of what is now 166 Road about ¼ mile west of D Road (Ref 78) [39°20'50.8"N 95°59'00.6"W]. Note that neither D Road nor 166 Road currently go through this area. This was likely a private school since High Prairie was not chartered until 1893.



1903:

The High Prairie schoolhouse was shown on the map in Adrian Township on the northwest corner of what is now the intersection of 158 Road and E Road (Ref 12). There is a house located there now, unknown whether it is a remodel of the original schoolhouse (Ref Google Maps 2025).
