1881:

Bateman school district was organized. It was split from Liberty school district. (Ref 89)


Apr 29, 1881:

The first meeting was held at the residence of J.E. Edwards. G.C. Bateman was elected secretary, J.C. Edwards director and J.C. Bateman treasurer. They voted to buy or lease one acre from Robert Weeks on the SE corner of the SE 1/4 of Section 22, Range 15 for $75.(Ref 89)


Aug 4, 1881:

The board voted to build a 24’ X 34’ schoolhouse with twelve foot ceilings.(Ref 89)
