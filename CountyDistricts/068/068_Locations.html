1881:

The Bateman schoolhouse was shown on the map in Liberty Township on the west side of Q4 Road (Old US 75) about 1/16th mile south of 254 Road (Ref 14) [39°30'21.6"N 95°43'47.1"W]. Note that the schoolhouse was located on a ¼ section owned by “GC Bateman” thus explaining the name.


Aug 4, 1881:

The board voted to build a 24’ X 34’ schoolhouse with twelve foot ceilings.(Ref 89)


1887:

The Bateman schoolhouse was in Liberty Township at the northwest corner of what is now 254 Road and Q4 Road (Old US 75) (Ref 15) [39°30'24.4"N 95°43'46.9"W].
