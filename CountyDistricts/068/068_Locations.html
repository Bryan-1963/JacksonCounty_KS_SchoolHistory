1881:

The Bateman schoolhouse was in Liberty Township on the west side of Q4 Road (Old US 75) about 1/16th mile south of 254 Road (Ref 14) [39°30'21.6"N 95°43'47.1"W]. Note that the schoolhouse was located on a ¼ section owned by “GC Bateman” thus explaining the name.


1887:

The Bateman schoolhouse was in Liberty Township at the northwest corner of what is now 254 Road and Q4 Road (Old US 75) (Ref 15) [39°30'24.4"N 95°43'46.9"W].


1903:

The Bateman schoolhouse was in Liberty Township at the northwest corner of what is now 254 Road and Q4 Road (Old US 75) (Ref 12) [39°30'24.4"N 95°43'46.9"W].


2025:

Nothing remains of the 1903 school today, but Bryan Stauffer remembers the building still being there in the late 1970s (Ref Google Maps 2025).
