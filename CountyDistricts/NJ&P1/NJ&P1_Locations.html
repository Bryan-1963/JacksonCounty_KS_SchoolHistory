1899:

The America City schoolhouse was shown on the Pottawatomie map as being in Nemaha County on the north side of what is now K63 Highway just west of where Cedar Bluff Road would intersect K63 if Cedar Bluff went straight north (Ref 55) [39°33'59.9"N 96°02'12.8"W].


1908:

The America City school house was within America City on the east side of what is now K Road (Nemaha County) just north of 286 Road (Jackson County) which is also KS 63 (Ref 16) [39°34'05.2"N 96°02'57.2"W].
