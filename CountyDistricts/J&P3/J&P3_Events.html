Sep 1905:

The Soldier, Kansas newspaper reported the following pupils for Buckeye Ridge: Virginia Wilson, Mabel Wilson, Claud Minner, Roxie Minner, Charlie Minner, Ratia Minner, Bertha Minner, Fred Kimble, Lota McKee, Eva Colher, Pearl Channel, Edith Channel, Estella Rutherford, Cleo Rutherford, Clyde Rutherford, Ira Rutherford, Clayburne Schoonover, Nellie Schoonover, Albert Schoonover, Edward Schoonover, Elvin Schoonover, Lindsey Drum. The teacher was Jennie Shepherd (Ref 54).


Dec 1905:

The Buckeye Ridge school reported 28 pupils enrolled with an average attendance of 19. Students mentioned for good attendance included Lota McKee, Ira McKee, Frank Thompson, Hobart Thompson, Evie Collier, Pearl Channell, Estella Rutherford, Cleo Rutherford, Linza Drum, Clyde Rutherford, Ira Rutherford. They had five visitors and the teacher was Jennie Shepherd (Ref 53).


Apr 7, 1910

The Holton Signal shows this district under Jackson County supervision.
