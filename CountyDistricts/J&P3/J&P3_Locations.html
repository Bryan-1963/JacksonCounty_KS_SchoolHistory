1881:

The Buckeye Ridge schoolhouse was in Soldier Township on the east side of what is now A Road just under ½ mile north of 262 Road (Ref 14) [39°31'42.0"N 96°02'07.6"W].


1887:

The Buckeye Ridge schoolhouse was in Soldier Township on the east side of what is now A Road just under ½ mile north of 262 Road (Ref 15) [39°31'42.0"N 96°02'07.6"W].


1899:

The Grant Township, Pottawatomie County map for 1899 shows ’Jt. SD 3’ on the edge of what is now Jackson County A Road but not the schoolhouse location (Ref 55). It was likely on the east side of A Road in Jackson County, which correlates with the location on the 1912 district boundaries map and the 1881 and 1887 Jackson County maps.


1903:

The Jackson County map does not show this schoolhouse (Ref 12).


1912:

The district boundaries map shows the schoolhouse in Soldier Township, (Township 6, Range 13 East, Section 13) which would place it on the east side of A Road between 262 Road and 270 Road (Ref 12).


2025:

There is no evidence of any remains of any structure there on modern aerial maps and a street view was not available (Ref Google Maps 2025).
