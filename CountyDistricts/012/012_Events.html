Nov 5, 1859:

North Star school district was organized. The first meeting to organize was held in the Point Pleasant schoolhouse, which is what this district was called in board meeting minutes until 3/28/1872. (Ref 89) NOTE: how does this reconcile with district 13?


Mar 28, 1872:

School board minutes began referring to the district name as “Star.” Alpheus Bainbridge was board chairman and Alfred Richards was clerk.(Ref 89)


1885:

There were 88 pupils.(Ref 89)


1886:

The district was split due to population increases. A new building for North Star was built and the other half became South Star.(Ref 89)
