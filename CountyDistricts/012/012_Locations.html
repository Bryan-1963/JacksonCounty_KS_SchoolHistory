1878:

The 1878 map shows a schoolhouse on the north side of what would now be 110 Road, if 110 Road went through here, and almost ½ mile west of X Road (Ref 78). [39°14'45.7"N 95°37'00.1"W]


Figure 1 –1878 Map Location of North Star #12 Schoolhouse (Ref 78)


1881:

The North Star schoolhouse was shown on the map in southeastern Douglas Township on the east side of what is now X Road a little under half a mile south of 114 Road (Ref 14) [39°14'44.0"N 95°36'29.8"W].


Figure 2 –1881 Map Location of North Star #12 Schoolhouse (Ref 14)


1883:

The 1883 map shows a school on the north side of what would now be 110 Road about ¼ mile west of X Road (Ref 81) [39°14'45.4"N 95°36'44.8"W]


Figure 3 –1883 Map Location of North Star #12 Schoolhouse (Ref 81)


1887:

The North Star schoolhouse was shown on the map in southeastern Douglas Township on the northeast corner of what would now be the intersection of 110 Road and X Road if 110 Road went through (Ref 15) [39°14'44.0"N 95°36'29.8"W].


Figure 4 –1887 Map Location of North Star #12 Schoolhouse (Ref 15)


1903:

The North Star schoolhouse was shown on the map in southeaster Douglas Township on the west side of what is now X Road a little under half a mile north of 114 Road (Ref 12) [39°15'34.0"N 95°36'29.2"W].


Figure 5 –1903 Map Location of North Star #12 Schoolhouse (Ref 12)

1963:

North Star is not identified on the 1963 map (Ref 83).


2025:
