1878:

The map shows a schoolhouse in Cedar Township on the northwest corner of what is now the intersection of 174 Road and X Road if 174 Road went through (Ref 78) [39°21'41.1"N 95°35'55.8"W].


Figure 1 – 1878 Map with Cedar Center School #58 (Ref 78)


1881:

The Cedar Center schoolhouse was shown on the map in eastern Cedar Township almost 1/8th mile west of what is now X Road a little over 1 mile north of 170 Road (Ref 14) [39°22'06.4"N 95°35'58.8"W].


1887:

The Cedar Center schoolhouse was shown on the map in eastern Cedar Township on the west side of what is now X Road a little over 1 mile north of 170 Road (Ref 15) [39°22'07.1"N 95°35'55.3"W]. Note that the scale of the 1887 map does not allow definitive proof of a difference from the 1881 and 1903 location.


1903:

The Cedar Center schoolhouse was shown on the map in eastern Cedar Township on the west side of what is now X Road a little over a mile north of 170 Road (Ref 12) [39°22'06.4"N 95°35'58.8"W].


2025:

There are no remains evident on modern aerial maps (Ref Google Maps 2025).
