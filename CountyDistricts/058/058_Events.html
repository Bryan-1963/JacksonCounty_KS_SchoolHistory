1877:

The Moore school was built. The teacher was Jane Gillieck.(Ref 89)


1877-1909:

Teacher salaries ranged from $30 to $45 per month.(Ref 89)


Mar 19, 1892:

The Holton Recorder listed “Moore School” district as 81 (Stoney Point).


Nov 9, 1905 & Feb 1, 1906:

The Holton Recorder listed “Moore School” district as 58


1909?:

Due to population shifts, the school was sold to George Moore for $25.(Ref 89)


Sep 1909:

The first Cedar Center schoolhouse was completed. Everything in the building was new except the organ. There were 28 desks and 22 pupils.(Ref 89)


1910:

A new bell was purchased. The bell was paid for with about $10 of proceeds from a box supper and $5.54 from the school board. The bell was 24” in diameter and weighed 250 pounds, outclassing Denison’s 18” bell.(Ref 89)


1917:

The schoolhouse was destroyed by a tornado. A new schoolhouse was built.(Ref 89)


May 2, 1918” The Holton Recorder listed District 58 as “Cedar Hill”
1950:

The district was disorganized. Bernard Kennedy bought the school.(Ref 89)
