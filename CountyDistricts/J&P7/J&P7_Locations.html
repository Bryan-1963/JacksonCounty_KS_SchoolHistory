1899:

The English Ridge school house was in Grant Township, Pottawatomie County on the west side of what is now English Ridge Road almost ½ mile south of Shove Road (Ref 55) [39°29'10.0"N 96°02'42.3"W]. There is a farmhouse and outbuildings in that location today.


2025:

There are no obvious remains of the schoolhouse visible on modern aerial maps, and a street view was not available (Ref Google Maps 2025).
