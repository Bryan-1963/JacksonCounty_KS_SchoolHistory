1878:

Eureka School was in Netawaka Township on the northwest corner of what would be the intersection of Q Road and 286 Road if Q Road existed in this location (Ref 15) [39°33'58.0"N 95°43'57.6"W]. Note that US75 is designated as Q Road here but is not on the section line where Q Road should be north of the parallel road. US75 aligns with Q4 road south of the parallel here.


Figure 1 –1878 Location of Eureka #18 Schoolhouse (Ref 78)


1881:

The Eureka School was not shown on the 1881 map (Ref 14).


1887:

Eureka School was in Netawaka Township on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of Q4 Road (Hwy 75) and 286 Road (Ref 15) [39°33'58.0"N 95°43'57.6"W].


1903:

Eureka School was in Netawaka Township on the east side of what is now Q4 Road (Hwy 75) and about 1/4 mile north of 286 Road (Ref 12) [39°34'07.7"N 95°43'56.7"W].
