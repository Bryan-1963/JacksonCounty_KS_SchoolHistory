1878:

The Parallel schoolhouse was shown on the map in Whiting Township somewhat northeast of what is now the intersection of 286 Road (First Standard Parallel South) and X Road (Ref 78) [39°34'04.0"N 95°35'58.1"W]. However, the scale and resolution of the 1878 map does not rule out that this could be the same location as on later maps.


Figure 1 –1878 Location of Parallel #43 Schoolhouse (Ref 78)


1881:

The Parallel schoolhouse was shown on the map in Whiting Township on the north side of what is now 286 Road (First Standard Parallel South) about ¼ mile east of X Road (Ref 14) [39°33'57.8"N 95°35'51.4"W].


1887:

The Parallel schoolhouse was shown on the map in Whiting Township on the north side of what is now 286 Road (First Standard Parallel South) about ¼ mile east of X Road (Ref 15) [39°33'57.8"N 95°35'51.4"W].


1903:

The Parallel schoolhouse was shown on the map in Whiting Township on the north side of what is now 286 Road (First Standard Parallel South) about ¼ mile east of X Road (Ref 12) [39°33'57.8"N 95°35'51.4"W].


2025:

There are no apparent remains of the schoolhouse today at that location (Ref Google Maps 2025).
