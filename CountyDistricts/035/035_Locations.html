1881:

The South Cedar schoolhouse was shown on the map in Cedar Township on the southwest corner of what is now the intersection of 166 Road and T Road (Ref 14) [39°20'48.7"N 95°40'55.1"W].


Dec 19, 1883:

The Holton Signal reported that South Cedar school would soon be completed and ready for school. But there was already a schoolhouse in 1876 and 1881?


Dec 22, 1886:

The Holton Signal reported that the South Cedar School “located about a mile and a half east of Mayetta” was being taught by Prof. A.E. Crane. This closely matches the 1887 map location.


1887:

The South Cedar schoolhouse was shown on the map in Cedar Township on the southeast corner of what is now the intersection of 166 Road and S Road (Ref 15) [39°20'47.8"N 95°42'03.0"W]. The 1881 and 1903 location of the South Cedar schoolhouse is occupied on this map by an M.E. Church.


Mar 21, 1895:

The Holton Recorder reported that Robert Miller was building a barn just north of South Cedar schoolhouse on the ¼ section his father bought from R. Boling. Note that on the 1903 map (Ref 12), a ¼ section belonging to J.H. Miller was located on the northwest corner of the intersection of what is now S Road and 166 Road. This corresponds to the 1887 map location of South Cedar School, if any credence can be leant to the relationship of “Robert Miller” and “J.H. Miller” separated by eight years.


Sep 26, 1895:

The Holton Recorder reported that O. J. Larkin was assisting Mr. Thompson with putting a white coat of plastering on the South Cedar schoolhouse.


Oct 9, 1902:

The Holton Recorder reported that H. Chase of Holton was putting an iron bridge over the South Cedar creek 1 ½ miles north of the South Cedar school house. On Oct 23, 1902 the Holton Recorder reported the bridge was complete (repeating the location as 1 ½ miles north of the South Cedar schoolhouse) and listed people who had assisted in building it. The 1887 map location is 1 ½ miles south of South Cedar creek whereas the 1903 and-on location is less than 3/4 mile south of South Cedar creek.


Nov 6, 1902:

The Holton Recorder reported that John Laird and C. D. Haven purchased the Pat Mathews 80 (acres) located ½ mile south of South Cedar school. The 1903 map (Ref 12) shows that C.D. Haven owned 80 acres on the east side of what is now S Road from 158 Road to 162 Road. This further bolsters South Cedar's location at the 1887 map location.


Jan 15, 1903:

The Holton Recorder reported that school district No. 35 was building a new schoolhouse one mile east of the old school and on the former location of the South Cedar M.E. Church.


1903:

The South Cedar schoolhouse was shown on the map in Cedar Township on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of 166 Road and T Road (Ref 12). [39°20'48.7"N 95°40'55.1"W].


Aug 24, 1904:

The Mayetta News reported that the Republicans would meet at South Cedar schoolhouse, two miles east of Mayetta. This aligns with the 1903-2025 location of the schoolhouse.


1969:

The South Cedar schoolhouse was shown on the map in Cedar Township on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of 166 Road and T Road and was being used as the Mayetta Kindergarten (Ref 68). The photo below shows the front of the school along with the hand pump well [39°20'48.7"N 95°40'55.1"W].
