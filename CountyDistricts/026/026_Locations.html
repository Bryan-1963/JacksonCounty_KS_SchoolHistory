1878:

The map shows a school in Soldier Township on the west side of what would be E Road (if it went straight through) and halfway between 270 Road and 278 Road (Ref 78) [39°33'28.5"N 95°57'43.2"W]. This would put it on the west side of KS62 and ½ mile south of 278 Road.



1811:

The schoolhouse was shown on the map in Soldier Township on the west side of what is now KS62 in the east half of what is now Soldier Cemetery (Ref 14).


1903:

The Soldier Schoolhouse was located on the southwest corner of Francis Street and 2nd Street (Ref 12).



1921:

The grade school was located on the southwest corner of Francis Street and 2nd Street and the high school was located on the northeast corner of Friend Street and 2nd Street (Ref 10).
