1881:

The Woodworth schoolhouse was shown on the map in Liberty Township on the northeast corner of what is now 278 Road and R Road (Ref 14). [39°33'00.9"N 95°43'11.3"W]


1887:

The Woodworth schoolhouse was shown on the map in Liberty Township on the south side of what is now 270 Road a little over ½ mile west of S Road (Ref 15) [39°32'07.1"N 95°42'41.3"W]. Note that Reference 15 also incorrectly shows District 27 in the location of District 72.


1903:

The Woodworth schoolhouse was shown on the map in Liberty Township on the south side of what is now 270 Road a little over ½ mile west of S Road (Ref 12) [39°32'07.1"N 95°42'41.3"W].


2025:

No remains are evident on modern aerial maps at any of the Woodworth schoolhouse locations (Ref Google Maps 2025).
