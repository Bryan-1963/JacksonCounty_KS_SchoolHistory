11/26/1926:

Several of the children were attending West Powhattan School and had no tardies nor absences in the third month:



Teacher: Elva Plankinton

Students: Margaret Field, Mary Field, William Field, Fay Bontrager, Roy Bontrager, Clayton Bontrager, Harry Bontrager, Thelma Bontrager, Lynn Moore, Lee Noe, Gladys Roush, Galen Roush, Harry Meers, Helen Meers, Edna Meers, Ruby Porter, Bill Porter, Edwin Porter.
