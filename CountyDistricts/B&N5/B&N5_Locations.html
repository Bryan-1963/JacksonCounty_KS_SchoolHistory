1887:

The West Powhattan school house was in Powhattan Township, Brown County at the northwest corner of what is now 110th Street and Antelope Road (Ref 61) [39°40'04.0"N 95°46'13.1"W].


1919:

The West Powhattan school house was in Powhattan Township, Brown County at the northwest corner of what is now 110th Street and Antelope Road (Ref 49). Remains of the foundation are visible on modern aerial maps and street views (Ref Google Maps 2025) [39°40'04.0"N 95°46'13.1"W].
