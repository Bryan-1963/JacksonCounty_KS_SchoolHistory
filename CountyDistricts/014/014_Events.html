1859:

Cedar Vale school district was organized (Ref 89).


Aug 13, 1903:

Holton Recorder lists the teacher at Cedar Vale for the coming year as being C.R. Peters


Aug 18, 1904:

Holton Recorder lists the teacher at Cedar Vale for the coming year as being C.R. Peters


Sep 19, 1904:

Holton Tribune reported “The schoolhouse in Cedar Vale is about completed and ready for school. Mr. Charles Kyder and August Winters has just finished giving it a beautiful painting.”


Sep 23, 1904:

Holton Recorder noted C.R. Peters was starting his third year as teacher at Cedar Vale


Sep 7, 1905:

Holton Recorder lists the teacher at Cedar Vale for the coming year as being Henrietta Heyman


Jul 26, 1906:

Holton Signal lists the teacher at Cedar Vale for the coming year as being Henrietta Heyman


Jul 25, 1907:

Holton Signal lists the teacher at Cedar Vale for the coming year as being Myrtle Coleman


Jul 30, 1908:

Holton Signal lists the teacher at Cedar Vale for the coming year as being Etta Hafer


Aug 12, 1908:

Holton Signal lists the teacher at Cedar Vale for the coming year as being Jennie E. Neibling


Dec 2, 1909:

Holton Recorder, in the “Mayetta Section” noted a box social held at the Cedar Dale schoolhouse with the teacher listed as Miss Jennie Neibling.


Apr 7, 1910:

The Holton Signal listed the Cedar Dale school as having:

  • 1909 Taxable valuation of $160,560
  • 1909 Levy in mills of 3.56
  • 1909 School Census of 43
  • 1908-1909 Enrollment of 30
  • 1908-1909 Avg Daily attendance of 23
  • 1909-1910 Months in Term of 7
  • 1909 Term began Sep. 6
  • Value of school property of $1300
  • Balances on hand June 30, 1909 of $118.99
  • Salary of teacher, per month, 1909-1910 of $47.50

May 26, 1910:

The Mayetta Herald reported four new graduates of Cedar Vale: Mamie Jones, Hazel Laird, Charlie Landon and Russell Gooderl


Aug 25, 1910:

The Holton Signal reported that Jennie Neibling would be the teacher for 1910-1911 school year


Dec 1, 1910:

Holton Recorder reported on a box social “at Cedarvale schoolhouse, two miles north of Mayetta.” This would be the location at the SW corner of what is now 178 and R Roads
