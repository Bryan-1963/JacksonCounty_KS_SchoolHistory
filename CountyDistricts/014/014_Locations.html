1878:

The map shows Cedar Vale schoolhouse located in Cedar Township on the west side of what is now S Road and nearly ½ mile south of 190 Road (Ref 78) [39°23'00.0"N 95°42'04.6"W].



1881:

The Cedar Vale schoolhouse was located at the southeast corner of the intersection of what is now 182 Road and R4 Road (Ref 14).


1901:

The Cedar Vale schoolhouse was built two miles north of Mayetta. (Ref 89). This would be the location at the southwest corner of what is now the intersection of 178 Road and R Road, however, the 1903 map still showed it he northeast corner of the intersection of 182 Road and R4 Road. It's possible the map was not updated?


1903:

The Cedar Vale schoolhouse was located at what is now the northeast corner of the intersection of 182 Road and R4 Road (Ref 12).


12/1/1910:

The Holton Recorder reported on a box social ’at Cedarvale schoolhouse, two miles north of Mayetta.’ This would be the location at the southwest corner of what is now the intersection of 178 Road and R Road. The school was thus relocated to this location and/or built new in this location sometime between 1903 and 1910 but the exact date is not now known.

1966-1980:

The foundation of the school at the southwest corner of what is now the intersection of 178 Road and R Road was still visible. Eventually the foundation was removed to expand the usable area of the pasture.


2025:

Nothing remains at any of the locations of the schoolhouse (Ref Google Maps 2025).
