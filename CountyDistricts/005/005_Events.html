1859:

Banner school district #5 was organized. The original district included 15 sections (square miles). Some of this territory was later detached to Maple Grove, Pleasant Valley, Sunflower and Winding Vale. (Ref 89)


3/25/1869:

The southwest quarter of section 35, township 7, range 14, was attached to Banner district #5. (Ref 4)


1883:

There were 85 different students. There were 136 days of school with an average attendance of 46. (Ref 89)


1884:

There were 65 different students with an average attendance of 31. The students ranged in age from five to twenty years old.(Ref 89)
