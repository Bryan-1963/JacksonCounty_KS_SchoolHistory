1903:

The Stoney Point schoolhouse was shown on the map in western Washington Township on the east side of what is now E Road and ¼ mile north of 118 Road (Ref 12). The concrete front steps and porch remain at the site in 2025 (Ref Google Maps 2025).






May 2, 1918 The Holton Signal reported that Arthur Voekel would be the teacher for the 1918-1919 school term.

Vinita Gwendolyn Linsacum (Stauffer) attended this school for a short time

Anna Mae Westrand, Ruth Eleanor McClain (Whittington, Westrand), George Eugene Eakin, Alice Osburn (Shuck, Carmen) at George and Stacia Eakin home NE of Delia with Stony Point School just visible in thebackground to the left of Anna Mae Westrand.
