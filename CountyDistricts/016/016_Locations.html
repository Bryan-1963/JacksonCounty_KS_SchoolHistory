1865:

A white limestone building was constructed to be a Methodist Episcopal Seminary. It was never used as a seminary and was used as the Circleville public schoolhouse from 1868 to 1919 (Ref 46).

Notes:

  1. The start date here is different than Ref 11 which said 1880, rather than 1868 for the public school district to take over the seminary building.
  2. The new school was built in 1911 so it’s unclear why the seminary building would have continued to be used until 1919.



After 1880:

Circleville School District #16 took over the Methodist Seminary Building after 1880 and used it until the brick school was built in 1911 (Ref 11).


1881:

The 1881 map does not identify the location of the Circleville schoolhouse (Ref 14).


1903:

The 1903 map does not identify the location of the Circleville schoolhouse (Ref 12). However, a building is shown at the probable seminary location as a black rectangle at the north end of Seminary (Street) on the north side of McKinley Avenue (Ref 12).


1911:

The brick high school was built (Ref 11). It had a semi-basement and two floors above that. High School was held on the top floor plus two classrooms in the semi-basement for typing and health/home economics. A shop and restrooms were added to the north side of the high school sometime after 1911. The middle floor was the elementary school. The basement also had the cafeteria and the furnace room and provided access to the restrooms and shop (Ref 13). 1929:

The gymnasium was built (Ref 11). The brick high school and gymnasium were used until school unification in 1967 (Ref 11). The gymnasium continues to be used for community events to this day.







2025:

No remains of the schoolhouse on Seminary Street are visible today (Ref Google Maps 2025).
