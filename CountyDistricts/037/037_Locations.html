1881:

The Wobourn Schoolhouse was shown on the map in western Douglas Township and was located on the northeast corner of what is now 110 Road and O4 Road (Ref 14) [39°14'44.4"N 95°45'58.0"W].


1887:

The Wobourn schoolhouse was shown on the map in western Douglas Township and was located on the north side of what is now 110 Road, a little over ½ mile west of P4 Road. This would be the northwest corner of the intersection of 110 Road and P Road if P Road existed there (Ref 15) [39°14'44.0"N 95°45'26.4"W].


1903:

The Wobourn Schoolhouse was shown on the map in southwestern Douglas Township and was located on the north side of what is now 110 Road, a little over ½ mile west of P4 Road. This would be the northwest corner of the intersection of 110 Road and P Road if P Road existed there (Ref 12) [39°14'44.0"N 95°45'26.4"W].


2025:

There is no evidence of any remains visible on modern aerial maps of the area (Ref Google Maps 2025).
