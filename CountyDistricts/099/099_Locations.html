8/31/1922:

The Holton Recorder reported that a new schoolhouse was to be built three miles west of Witchewah and would be called Grand Prairie.


4/19/1923:

The Holton Recorder reported the organization of the Grand Prairie M.E. Sunday School at the ’new schoolhouse northeast of Blandin.’

The Grand Prairie schoolhouse was shown on the map in XX Township on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of 174 Road and J Road (Ref 13, Google Maps 2025). The house that is located there could be a remodel of the schoolhouse (Ref Google Maps 2025). Auswell Stauffer bought the swing set that was made of old car frames and used it to build the gas tank stand for his father-in-law, George Eakin. The tank and stand were located on George’s farm immediately east of the Delia Cemetery (Ref 13).
