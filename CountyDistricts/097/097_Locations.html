1918:

The Witchewa schoolhouse was shown on the map in XX Township on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of 174 Road and L Road (Ref 27). No remains are visible there on modern aerial maps (Ref Google Maps 2025).




1921:

The Witchewa schoolhouse was shown on a 1921 map on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of 174 Road and M Road (Ref 10). This is a mile east of the 1918 location.
