1878:

A school was shown on the map in Jefferson Township northwest of the intersection of what is now 270 Road and M Road (Ref 78). This may have been a private school before the creation of District #63.


Figure 1 –1878 Location of Pea Ridge #63 (Ref 78)


1881:

The Pea Ridge schoolhouse was in Jefferson Township on the north side of what is now 278 Road about 3/8 of a mile east of L Road (Ref 14). [39°33'02.4"N 95°49'20.9"W]


1887:

The Pea Ridge schoolhouse was in Jefferson Township on the north side of what is now 278 Road about 3/8 of a mile east of L Road (Ref 14). [39°33'02.4"N 95°49'20.9"W]


1903:

The Pea Ridge schoolhouse was in Jefferson Township on the north side of what is now 278 Road about ½ mile east of L Road (Ref 12). [39°33'02.4"N 95°49'17.2"W]


2025:

No remains are visible on modern aerial maps (Ref Google Maps 2025). Note that the difference in locations between 1881 and 1903 could have been errors in making the maps.
