This school was located within the city limits of Netawaka, in the Township of Netawaka. The building was on the southeast corner of the intersection of Morgan and Chestnut Streets just east of where the baseball field is located today (Ref 12). Nothing remains of the building in the 2020s (Ref Google Maps 2025).

A new, larger schoolhouse was built (Ref 86).
