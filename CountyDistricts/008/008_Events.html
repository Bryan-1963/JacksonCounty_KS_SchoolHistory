Aug 24, 1859:

Pleasant Valley school district was organized (Ref 7).


Mar 8, 1868:

The school was called the Burnett school since the location chosen was on the corner of the Burnett farm (Ref 7).


ca 1872:

The school was renamed to Pleasant Valley after a suggestion from Mrs. T.A. McCart (Ref 7).


1883:

Total expense for the school year was $242.59 (Ref 7).


1887:

The teacher salary was $40 per month (Ref 7).


1894:

The teacher salary had dropped to $25 per month (Ref 7).


1907-1908:

The budget for the year was $590.46 and actual expenditure was $562.25. The school was insured for $800 with a $28 premium (Ref 7).


1923:

Total expense for the year was $942.77. Older girls in the school prepared hot lunches. Subjects taught included literature, physiology, music, Kansas History, two levels of U.S. History, geography, civics, writing and arithmetic (Ref 7).


1920s:

Teacher salaries ranged from $70 per month to $110 per month (Ref 7).


Depression:

Teacher salaries plunged to $50 per month (Ref 7).


1933:

The district expenses totaled $503. The school had a 40 volume library and four new books were purchased for $3.44 (Ref 7).


1943:

Total expenses for the year were $1050.58 (Ref 7).


1944:

The teacher salary was $120 per month (Ref 7).


1948:

The teacher salary was $167.50 per month (Ref 7).


1952:

The teacher salary was $250 per month (Ref 7).


1953:

The total expenses for the year were $2873.85 (Ref 7).


1959- 1960:

The last term of school was held at Pleasant Valley. Total expenditures were $5044.71 (Ref 7).


1963:

District #8 was dissolved, and the schoolhouse and property were sold. Earl Askren, who owned the property surrounding the lot purchased the property and converted the schoolhouse into a private residence (Ref 7).
