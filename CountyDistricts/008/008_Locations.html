1859:

The original school district boundaries are shown below (Ref 7). The location of the schoolhouse is unknown.



Mar 8, 1868:

A site was designated for the location of a new school building on the northwest corner of the northeast corner of Section 9, Township7, Range 14 (Ref 7). This places the schoolhouse in the same location as shown on the 1881 map. A special meeting on May 17, 1868 determined the school building should be 20 x 26 feet in size (Ref 7). The school was called the Burnett school since the location chosen was on the corner of the Burnett farm (Ref 7).



1878:

Pleasant Valley schoolhouse was shown on the map in eastern Grant Township on the south side of what is now 230 Road (KS 16) and ¼ mile east of J Road (Ref 14) [39°27'46.8"N 95°51'46.8"W].




1881:

Pleasant Valley schoolhouse was shown on the map in eastern Grant Township on the south side of what is now 230 Road (KS 16) and a little over ½ mile east of J Road (Ref 14) [39°27'47.6"N 95°51'31.2"W].



1883:

Pleasant Valley schoolhouse was shown on the map in eastern Grant Township on the south side of what is now 230 Road (KS 16) and about ¼ mile east of J Road (Ref 81) [39°27'46.8"N 95°51'46.8"W].



1887:

Pleasant Valley schoolhouse was shown on the map in eastern Grant Township on the south side of what is now 230 Road (KS 16) and a little over ½ mile east of J Road (Ref 14) [39°27'47.6"N 95°51'31.2"W].



Jan? 1894:

Classes began in the new schoolhouse that was built on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of 230 Road (KS 16). The schoolhouse had a belfry and a partial basement (Ref 7). The original 1868 schoolhouse was converted into a house and Fred Fisher’s hired hand lived in it (Ref 13).


1903:

Pleasant Valley schoolhouse was shown on the map in eastern Grant Township on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of 230 Road (KS 16) and J Road (Ref 12) [39°27'48.6"N 95°52'00.6"W]. The woodburning stove in the classroom was replaced by a circulating heating system that burned coal (Ref 7).



Summer 1904:

A truancy law pushed the enrollment to 62 and an addition was made to the south end of the building (Ref 7)


Mar 1, 1912:

The school district boundaries were as shown on the map below. The schoolhouse was located on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of 230 Road (KS 16) and J Road (Ref 82) [39°27'48.6"N 95°52'00.6"W].



ca1912:



The girls’ outhouse in the northeast corner of the property is visible, the boys’ outhouse was in the northwest corner.
1921:

Pleasant Valley schoolhouse was shown on the map in eastern Grant Township on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of 230 Road (KS 16) and J Road (Ref 10) [39°27'48.6"N 95°52'00.6"W].



Late 1940s: A partition reaching 2/3 of the way to the ceiling was put in to separate the cloakroom/washroom entrance from the main classroom (Ref 7).


Feb 27, 1947:

The school district was expanded to the south and west to include portions of dissolved school districts #62 and #85 (Ref 7).



Expansion shown in diagonal hatching.
1950s:

In the early 1950s, a gas furnace was installed in place of the coal burning furnace (Ref 7).


The merry go round spun and tilted and it would spark at night. It is now at Don Fisher's place (2022). Kathryn Linehan's (teacher) 1951 Chevy Fleetline car is visible at the right.

Running cold water, a refrigerator for milk and a small cooking stove were installed during the mid 1950s.


Aug 15, 1959:

The school district was further expanded after the dissolution of District #20 (Ref 7):



Expansion shown in horizontal hatching.
1960:

The last classes were held in the building (Ref 7).


1962:

The 1868 schoolhouse was moved around 1962 when Hwy 16 went through and became an outbuilding on the farm of Robert Lyle Mannell (Ref 7). In 2025 it was still located there.



1963:

The district was dissolved, and the land and schoolhouse were sold to Earl Askren. Earl Askren converted the schoolhouse to a home (Ref 7). Pleasant Valley schoolhouse was shown on the map in eastern Grant Township on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of 230 Road (KS 16) and J Road (Ref 83) [39°27'48.6"N 95°52'00.6"W].
