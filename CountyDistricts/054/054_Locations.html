1881:

The Delia schoolhouse was in western Washington Township on the north side of what is now 102 Road about ¼ mile east of D Road (Ref 14) [39°13'52.6"N 95°58'32.6"W]. This was before Delia became a town in 1905 and is ½ mile south and ¾ mile west of Delia today.


1887:

The Delia schoolhouse was in western Washington Township on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of E Road and 102 Road (Ref 15) [39°13'52.2"N 95°57'38.9"W]. This was before Delia became a town in 1905 and is ½ mile south of Delia today.


1903:

The Delia schoolhouse was in western Washington Township on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of E Road and 102 Road (Ref 12) [39°13'52.2"N 95°57'38.9"W]. This was before Delia became a town in 1905 and is ½ mile south of Delia today.


Also called the "Murry" school (Ref 89).


1908

The 'new' grade school, district 54, was built within the city limits of Delia at 402 Nora Avenue having outgrown the old (Murry?) school that was south of town. This building was in use from 1908-1928.
