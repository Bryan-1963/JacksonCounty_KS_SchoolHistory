After 1947:

After dissolution of the district in 1947, the school building was cut in half and used to build two different barns (Ref 13) [39°27'48.0"N 95°55'23.9"W].


2025:

There are no remains of the school at the original location today. The barns built from the two halves of the schoolhouse are visible from KS16 (230 Road) to the northwest and southwest of the intersection with J Road (Ref 13) [39°27'48.0"N 95°55'23.9"W].
