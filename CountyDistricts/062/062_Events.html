5/23/1901:

Reference 41 contains photos of the 1901 Graduating Exercises program. Note that the pupils listed include some that were in the Banner school 1900-1901 so this may have been a multiple school 8th grade graduation event. Speakers and singers included Anna Hittle, Nellie Hood, Ethel Fairbank, Marie Morrow, Bertha Sawhill, Beatrice Elliott, Bessie Dulick, Dora Osborn. The diplomas were presented by Superintendent Whitcraft.
