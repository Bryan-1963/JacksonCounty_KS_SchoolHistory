1881:

The Browne Schoolhouse was shown on the map in western Washington Township on the south side of what is now 118 Road just under ½ mile east of C Road (Ref 14).


1903:

The Browne Schoolhouse was shown on the map in western Washington Township on the northeast corner of what is now the intersection of 126 Road and C Road (Ref 12).


2025:

No remains are visible on modern aerial maps (Ref Google Maps 2025).
