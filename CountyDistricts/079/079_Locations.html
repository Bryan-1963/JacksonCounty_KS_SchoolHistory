1971:

The spring of 1971 was the last usage of the brick schoolhouse at 2nd and Jones (Ref 68). Students transferred to the new elementary school at about 210 South 4th Street (Ref 68) [39°20'10.1"N 95°43'10.7"W].


1973:

Bud Stauffer built bleachers that were used for visitors at RVHS football games and in Mayetta for baseball games. Bud hauled them on his truck when seasons changed.





1975:

The photo below shows the Royal Valley Junior High School (foreground) with Mayetta Elementary School in the background (Ref 45). The Royal Valley Junior High building was formerly the Mayetta Rural High School. The fire escape shown is the very one used by Bryan Stauffer and the rest of Mr. Paradise’s history class the day the Junior High School burned down in 1977.




March 2, 1977:

The Royal Valley Junior High building (formerly Mayetta High School) burned down. All the students, teachers and staff successfully evacuated the building after the fire alarm went off. Classes continued that spring (and next year?) in the basement of the Catholic Church and in the former shop building that was east of the Junior High (Ref 68).
