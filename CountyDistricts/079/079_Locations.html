Jun 2, 1887:

The district selected lots 27, 29, 31 and 22 Jones Street, Lunger’s Addition for the school location (Ref 71). This location corresponds to the northeast corner of 2nd Street and Jones Street. This is now the location of the Mayetta Fire Department (Ref Google Maps 2025).



Aug 13, 1887:

The first schoolhouse was completed and turned over to the district (Ref 71).


1896:

An addition was made to the school building to enable two classrooms (Ref 71).

Aug 21, 1918:

The Mayetta Herald reported that the school board had let a contract for a temporary one room building between the schoolhouse and the Christian church (to the east of the schoolhouse) for high school classroom space. They anticipated waiting for the end of World War One to construct a new, permanent high school.
