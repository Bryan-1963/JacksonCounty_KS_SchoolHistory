Aug 21, 1918:

The Mayetta Herald reported that the high school teachers for this year were Professor Kelly, Miss Berkholder and Miss (Emma) Crabb.


1919:


Apr 16, 1919:

The Mayetta Herald reported that the annual Mayetta Rural High School district meeting was held. No change of the board members were made so George James continued as director, M. Fitzgerald as treasurer and W.M. Seibert as clerk. The present teaching staff was retained: Prof. Kelly at $1500 salary, Miss Burkholder and Miss (Emma) Crabb at $100 per month.


Apr 16, 1919:

The Mayetta Herald reported that the annual Mayetta city school board was held. The board was retained with no changes (F.L. Reynolds, J.M. Goheen, J.W. McMurray). Mrs. McCrory was retained as teacher with an increased salary, the remaining positions not yet filled.
