The Hazel Row school schoolhouse was shown on a map in Netawaka Township on the west side of what is now O Road ½ mile south of 302 Road (Ref 78). The 1878 map scale makes accuracy difficult, but this corresponds with the 1887 map location [39°35'15.9"N 95°46'13.9"W].
