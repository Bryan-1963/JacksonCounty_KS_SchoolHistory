1877:

The Stach schoolhouse was built and named after John Stach, who donated an acre of land for the site. The school educated children of Czech descent and served as a meeting place for the community (Ref 28).


1881:

The Stach schoolhouse was in southeastern Washington Township on the north side of what is now 110 Road just under ½ mile east of I Road (Ref 12) [39°14'44.6"N 95°52'40.6"W].


1887:

The Stach schoolhouse was in southeastern Washington Township on the south side of what is now 110 Road just under ½ mile east of I Road (Ref 12) [39°14'42.7"N 95°52'40.8"W].


1903:

The Stach schoolhouse was in eastern Washington Township on the north side of what is now 106 Road just under ½ mile east of I Road (Ref 12) [39°14'18.8"N 95°52'40.1"W].

1956:

The school was closed due to declining enrollment (Ref 28)


1983:

The Stach family donated the schoolhouse to the Kansas State Historical Society (Ref 28).


October 1984:

The schoolhouse was relocated to Topeka at the Kansas Historical Museum (Ref 28).



2025:

The schoolhouse is now located in Topeka at the Kansas Historical Museum. There are no remains at the original location (Ref Google Maps 2025).
