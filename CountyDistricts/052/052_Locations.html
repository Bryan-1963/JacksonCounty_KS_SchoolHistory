1881:

The Beauty Heights schoolhouse was in Jefferson Township on the south side of what is now 246 Road almost 1/8th mile east of I Road (Ref 14). This and the 1887 location were probably only different due to mapping accuracy. [39°29'32.5"N 95°53'07.5"W]


1887:

The Beauty Heights schoolhouse was in Jefferson Township on the southeast corner of what is now the intersection of 246 Road and I Road (Ref 15). This and the 1881 location were probably only different due to mapping accuracy. [39°29'32.6"N 95°53'11.0"W]
