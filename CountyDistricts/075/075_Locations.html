No obvious remains of the school are visible on modern aerial maps (Ref Google Maps 2025). At some point, the Pleasant View Schoolhouse was sold and moved to a location east of Circleville where it became a welding shop. This is on the north side of 254 Road about 1/8 mile west of L Road (Ref 13) [39°30'26.5"N 95°50'00.9"W].
