June 1915:

Stella McClain was listed as one of 118 students from across Jackson County who were enrolled in “The Jackson County Institute” that met at the Holton High School (Ref. 97, 98). The Institute was a set of classes and examinations to qualify teachers. At the end of July, Stella McClain received her third grade certificate and had the highest average of the thirteen who received that level (Ref. 99).
