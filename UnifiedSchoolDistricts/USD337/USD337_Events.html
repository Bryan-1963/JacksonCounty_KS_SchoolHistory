1968?

Mayetta and Hoyt combined to form Royal Valley Schools, USD 337. The grade schools remained separate in Mayetta and Hoyt. The Mayetta High School became Royal ValleyJunior High for the entire district and the Hoyt High School became Royal Valley High School. Mayetta kindergarten continued in the old South Cedar rural school building.


1970:

Kindergarten was relocated from South Cedar Schoolhouse to a mobile unit on the east side of 4th Street (R Road) south of James Street.

TO-DO: When did the former high school switch to Junior High?


1970-1971:

This was the last school year that the building at 2nd and Jones Street in Mayetta would be used.


1971:

The Mayetta Elementary School moved to a new building on the east side of 4th Street (R Road) south of James Street. The building there was later expanded and eventually became Royal Valley Junior High School.


1972?

The old Mayetta grade school at 2nd and Jones was razed



197x
