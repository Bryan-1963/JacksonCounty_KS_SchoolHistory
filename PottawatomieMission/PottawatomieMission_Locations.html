1881:

The mission was located within the Pottawatomi Reservation at about what is now on the south side of 124 Road about ¼ mile west of K Road (Ref 14) [39°16'12.3"N 95°51'30.7"W]. Note that there were no roads nor section lines laid out on the map of the reservation in 1881 so this location is approximate.


1880s:

1887:

The mission was located within the Pottawatomi Reservation at about what is now on 124 Road and about ½ mile east of J Road (Ref 15) [39°16'15.1"N 95°51'31.5"W]. This matches well with the current location of the remaining mission buildings.




1903:

The Mission school house was located within the Pottawatomi Reservation on the south side of what is now 124 Road about ¼ mile west of K Road (Ref 12) [39°16'12.9"N 95°51'15.4"W]. Note: Purely using coordinates from the 1903 map would put the schoolhouse much farther east of the other mission buildings and this map could have been off.
